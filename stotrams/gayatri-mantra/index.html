Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The most sacred mantra of the Vedas — a prayer to the divine light of Savitr (the Sun) to awaken and guide our minds.

When to recite

At sunrise, noon and sunset (sandhya times) — traditionally after learning it from a guru or elder.

The stotram

The stotram
oṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svaḥ tatsaviturvareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahi dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt ||
Meaning: Om. Earth, sky and heaven. We meditate on the most adorable, radiant light of the divine Savitr. May that light inspire and guide our minds.
