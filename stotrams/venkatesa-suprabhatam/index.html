Sri Venkatesa Suprabhatam

Prativadi Bhayankaram Annan (15th century) · Morning hymn to Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The famous dawn hymn beginning 'Kausalya supraja Rama'. The poet gently wakes the Lord of the Seven Hills, describing the morning: birds singing, sages bathing, devotees waiting at the door.

When to recite

Every morning before sunrise — it is sung daily at Tirumala to wake the Lord. Especially on Saturdays and in Dhanurmasam / Margazhi.

Meaning in simple words

O Rama, dear son of Kausalya, the eastern dawn is breaking; get up, the morning duties are to be done. O Govinda, rise; O Garuda-bannered Lord, rise and bless the three worlds. O Mother Lakshmi, beloved of Vishnu, ocean of compassion — a good morning to you. The sages bathing in the Ganga, the seven rishis at their morning prayers, the lotuses opening, the birds and bees singing, the gods and devotees waiting at the golden door — all this is described, and each verse ends 'Sheshadri-shekhara-vibho tava suprabhatam' — O Lord of the Seven Hills, may your morning be auspicious.
