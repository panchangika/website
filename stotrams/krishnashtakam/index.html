Krishnashtakam (Vasudeva Sutam Devam)

Traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Krishna

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Eight simple verses, each ending 'Krishnam vande jagadgurum' — I bow to Krishna, the teacher of the world. It walks through his story: son of Vasudeva and Devaki, joy of the gopis, friend of Arjuna.

When to recite

Janmashtami, Ekadashi, Wednesdays and every morning — it is short and easy for children.

Meaning in simple words

I bow to Krishna, the teacher of the world — son of Vasudeva, who killed Kamsa and Chanura and is the supreme joy of Devaki; who wears flowers of atasi, shining necklaces, bangles and anklets; whose curly hair and moon-like face are beautiful; who has peacock feathers, the beauty of crores of Manmathas; who sports with the gopis and holds the Kaustubha jewel; who is loved by Rukmini and is the charioteer and friend of Arjuna. Whoever recites this Ashtakam in the morning has the sins of crores of births destroyed by remembering Krishna.

The stotram

Verse 1
vasudevasutaṃ devaṃ kaṃsacāṇūramardanam | devakīparamānandaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 1 ||
Verse 2
atasīpuṣpasaṅkāśaṃ hāranūpuraśobhitam | ratnakaṅkaṇakeyūraṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 2 ||
Verse 3
kuṭilālakasaṃyuktaṃ pūrṇacandranibhānanam | vilasatkuṇḍaladharaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 3 ||
Verse 4
mandāragandhasaṃyuktaṃ cāruhāsaṃ caturbhujam | barhipiñchāvacūḍāṅgaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 4 ||
Verse 5
utphullapadmapatrākṣaṃ nīlajīmūtasannibham | yādavānāṃ śiroratnaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 5 ||
Verse 6
rukmiṇīkelisaṃyuktaṃ pītāmbarasuśobhitam | avāptatulasīgandhaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 6 ||
Verse 7
gopikānāṃ kucadvandvakuṅkumāṅkitavakṣasam | śrīniketaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 7 ||
Verse 8
śrīvatsāṅkaṃ mahoraskaṃ vanamālāvirājitam | śaṅkhacakradharaṃ devaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vande jagadgurum || 8 ||
Closing verse
kṛṣṇāṣṭakamidaṃ puṇyaṃ prātarutthāya yaḥ paṭhet | koṭijanmakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ smaraṇena vinaśyati ||
