Panchangika

Nirvana Shatakam (Shivoham)

Adi Shankaracharya · Six verses on the true Self

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Six verses in which the seeker says what he is NOT — not the mind, not the body, not the senses — and ends each verse with 'I am Shiva, pure consciousness and bliss'.

When to recite

Mondays, Shivaratri, and in quiet meditation at any time of the day.

The stotram

Verse 1
manobuddhyahaṅkāracittāni nāhaṃ na ca śrotrajihve na ca ghrāṇanetre | na ca vyoma bhūmirna tejo na vāyuḥ cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 1 ||
Meaning: I am not the mind, the intellect, the ego or the memory; not the ears, tongue, nose or eyes; not space, earth, fire or air. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
Verse 2
na ca prāṇasaṃjño na vai pañcavāyuḥ na vā saptadhāturna vā pañcakośaḥ | na vākpāṇipādaṃ na copasthapāyu cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 2 ||
Meaning: I am not the life-breath nor the five vital airs; not the seven tissues of the body nor its five sheaths; not speech, hands, feet or the other organs. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
Verse 3
na me dveṣarāgau na me lobhamohau mado naiva me naiva mātsaryabhāvaḥ | na dharmo na cārtho na kāmo na mokṣaḥ cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 3 ||
Meaning: I have no hatred or attachment, no greed or delusion, no pride and no jealousy; I am beyond duty, wealth, desire and even liberation. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
Verse 4
na puṇyaṃ na pāpaṃ na saukhyaṃ na duḥkhaṃ na mantro na tīrthaṃ na vedā na yajñāḥ | ahaṃ bhojanaṃ naiva bhojyaṃ na bhoktā cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 4 ||
Meaning: I have no merit or sin, no pleasure or pain; I need no mantra, pilgrimage, Veda or sacrifice; I am not the food, the eating or the eater. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
Verse 5
na me mṛtyuśaṅkā na me jātibhedaḥ pitā naiva me naiva mātā na janma | na bandhurna mitraṃ gururnaiva śiṣyaḥ cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 5 ||
Meaning: I have no fear of death and no divisions of caste; no father, no mother, no birth; no relative, friend, teacher or student. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
Verse 6
ahaṃ nirvikalpo nirākārarūpo vibhutvācca sarvatra sarvendriyāṇām | na cāsaṅgataṃ naiva muktirna meyaḥ cidānandarūpaḥ śivo'haṃ śivo'ham || 6 ||
Meaning: I am beyond all doubt and without form; I am everywhere, behind all the senses; I am never bound, so I need no freeing — I cannot be measured. I am Shiva — pure consciousness and bliss.
