Aditya Hrudayam

Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda · Taught by Sage Agastya to Sri Rama

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

On the battlefield, tired Rama faces Ravana. Sage Agastya comes and teaches him this 'heart of the Sun' hymn. Rama recites it three times, gains new strength and wins.

When to recite

Sundays at sunrise, facing the Sun; Ratha Saptami; before exams, interviews or any difficult task.

Meaning in simple words

Verses 1–5: Agastya tells Rama of this secret, eternal hymn that destroys enemies, brings victory, removes worry and grief, and increases life. Verses 6–15: the Sun is praised as the form of all gods — Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Skanda, Indra, Kubera, Yama, the Pitrs — and by many names: Aditya, Savitr, Surya, Pushan, the golden one, the giver of light and rain. Verses 16–20: salutations to him who rises on the eastern hill and sets on the western, the lord of light and of the day, who destroys darkness and cold. Verses 21–24: he creates and dissolves all beings, is awake in all while they sleep, and is the fruit of every sacrifice. Verses 25–31: anyone who praises him in danger or fear does not suffer; Rama recites it three times, looks at the Sun, takes up his bow, and the Sun, pleased, urges him to hurry and defeat Ravana.
