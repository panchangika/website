Navagraha Stotram

Attributed to Sage Vyasa · Hymn to the nine planets

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

One verse for each of the nine planets — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu — describing each one and bowing to it. Short, easy, and recited in many homes daily.

When to recite

Every day after the morning prayer; especially on Saturdays and when a planet is said to be troubling your horoscope.

The stotram

Verse 1
japākusumasaṅkāśaṃ kāśyapeyaṃ mahādyutim | tamo'riṃ sarvapāpaghnaṃ praṇato'smi divākaram || 1 ||
Meaning: I bow to the Sun, red like the hibiscus flower, son of Kashyapa, full of great light, enemy of darkness, who destroys all sins.
Verse 2
dadhiśaṅkhatuṣārābhaṃ kṣīrodārṇavasambhavam | namāmi śaśinaṃ somaṃ śambhormukuṭabhūṣaṇam || 2 ||
Meaning: I bow to the Moon, white like curd, the conch and snow, born from the ocean of milk, the jewel on Shiva's crown.
Verse 3
dharaṇīgarbhasambhūtaṃ vidyutkāntisamaprabham | kumāraṃ śaktihastaṃ taṃ maṅgalaṃ praṇamāmyaham || 3 ||
Meaning: I bow to Mars (Mangala), born from the Earth, shining like lightning, the young one who holds a spear.
Verse 4
priyaṅgukalikāśyāmaṃ rūpeṇāpratimaṃ budham | saumyaṃ saumyaguṇopetaṃ taṃ budhaṃ praṇamāmyaham || 4 ||
Meaning: I bow to Mercury (Budha), dark like the priyangu bud, of matchless beauty, gentle and full of gentle qualities.
Verse 5
devānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca guruṃ kāñcanasannibham | buddhibhūtaṃ trilokeśaṃ taṃ namāmi bṛhaspatim || 5 ||
Meaning: I bow to Jupiter (Brihaspati), teacher of the gods and sages, golden in colour, wisdom itself, lord of the three worlds.
Verse 6
himakundamṛṇālābhaṃ daityānāṃ paramaṃ gurum | sarvaśāstrapravaktāraṃ bhārgavaṃ praṇamāmyaham || 6 ||
Meaning: I bow to Venus (Shukra), white like snow, the jasmine and the lotus stalk, the great teacher of the asuras, who explains all the scriptures.
Verse 7
nīlāñjanasamābhāsaṃ raviputraṃ yamāgrajam | chāyāmārtaṇḍasambhūtaṃ taṃ namāmi śanaiścaram || 7 ||
Meaning: I bow to Saturn (Shani), dark like blue collyrium, son of the Sun and Chhaya, elder brother of Yama, who moves slowly.
Verse 8
ardhakāyaṃ mahāvīryaṃ candrādityavimardanam | siṃhikāgarbhasambhūtaṃ taṃ rāhuṃ praṇamāmyaham || 8 ||
Meaning: I bow to Rahu, who has only half a body, very powerful, who troubles the Moon and the Sun, born of Simhika.
Verse 9
palāśapuṣpasaṅkāśaṃ tārakāgrahamastakam | raudraṃ raudrātmakaṃ ghoraṃ taṃ ketuṃ praṇamāmyaham || 9 ||
Meaning: I bow to Ketu, red like the palasha flower, head of the stars and planets, fierce and powerful.
Closing verse
iti vyāsamukhodgītaṃ yaḥ paṭhetsusamāhitaḥ | divā vā yadi vā rātrau vighnaśāntirbhaviṣyati ||
Meaning: Whoever recites this hymn sung by Vyasa with a calm mind, by day or by night, will see their obstacles settle down.
