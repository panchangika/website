Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu (Devi Stuti)

From the Devi Mahatmyam (Durga Saptashati), chapter 5 · Hymn to the Goddess

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The gods praise the Goddess who lives in every living being — as intelligence, sleep, hunger, strength, patience, peace, faith, beauty, wealth, memory, compassion, contentment and, above all, as the Mother. Each line ends 'Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah'.

When to recite

All nine days of Navaratri, Fridays, Tuesdays and Durgashtami.

Meaning in simple words

'To the Goddess who dwells in all beings in the form of …, we bow to her, we bow to her, we bow to her again and again.' The forms named, in order, are: Vishnu's Maya, consciousness, intelligence, sleep, hunger, shadow (reflection), power, thirst, forgiveness, birth (kind), modesty, peace, faith, loveliness, prosperity (Lakshmi), activity, memory, compassion, contentment, the Mother, and illusion. The message is simple and deep: every quality we see in ourselves and in others is the Goddess herself.
