Madhurashtakam

Sri Vallabhacharya · Hymn to Lord Krishna

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Eight sweet verses saying that everything about Krishna is 'madhuram' — sweet: his lips, his walk, his flute, his friends, his cows. Every verse ends 'the Lord of Mathura is sweet in every way'.

When to recite

Janmashtami, Ekadashi, Wednesdays and any evening — a favourite for children to learn.

The stotram

Verse 1
adharaṃ madhuraṃ vadanaṃ madhuraṃ nayanaṃ madhuraṃ hasitaṃ madhuram | hṛdayaṃ madhuraṃ gamanaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 1 ||
Meaning: His lips are sweet, his face is sweet, his eyes are sweet, his smile is sweet, his heart is sweet, his walk is sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 2
vacanaṃ madhuraṃ caritaṃ madhuraṃ vasanaṃ madhuraṃ valitaṃ madhuram | calitaṃ madhuraṃ bhramitaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 2 ||
Meaning: His words are sweet, his deeds are sweet, his clothes are sweet, the way he turns is sweet, the way he moves and wanders is sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 3
veṇurmadhuro reṇurmadhuraḥ pāṇirmadhuraḥ pādau madhurau | nṛtyaṃ madhuraṃ sakhyaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 3 ||
Meaning: His flute is sweet, the dust of his feet is sweet, his hands and feet are sweet, his dance is sweet, his friendship is sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 4
gītaṃ madhuraṃ pītaṃ madhuraṃ bhuktaṃ madhuraṃ suptaṃ madhuram | rūpaṃ madhuraṃ tilakaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 4 ||
Meaning: His song is sweet, his yellow cloth is sweet, the way he eats and sleeps is sweet, his form is sweet, his tilak is sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 5
karaṇaṃ madhuraṃ taraṇaṃ madhuraṃ haraṇaṃ madhuraṃ smaraṇaṃ madhuram | vamitaṃ madhuraṃ śamitaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 5 ||
Meaning: His actions are sweet, the way he rescues us is sweet, the way he steals (butter and hearts) is sweet, his play is sweet, even his calm is sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 6
guñjā madhurā mālā madhurā yamunā madhurā vīcī madhurā | salilaṃ madhuraṃ kamalaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 6 ||
Meaning: His gunja-berry necklace and garland are sweet, the Yamuna and its waves are sweet, its water and lotuses are sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 7
gopī madhurā līlā madhurā yuktaṃ madhuraṃ muktaṃ madhuram | dṛṣṭaṃ madhuraṃ śiṣṭaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 7 ||
Meaning: The gopis are sweet, his divine play is sweet, his meeting and his parting are sweet, his glance and his manners are sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
Verse 8
gopā madhurā gāvo madhurā yaṣṭirmadhurā sṛṣṭirmadhurā | dalitaṃ madhuraṃ phalitaṃ madhuraṃ madhurādhipaterakhilaṃ madhuram || 8 ||
Meaning: The cowherd boys are sweet, the cows are sweet, his stick is sweet, his whole creation is sweet, what he breaks and what he makes fruitful are sweet. Everything about the Lord of Mathura is sweet.
