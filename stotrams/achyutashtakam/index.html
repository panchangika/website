Panchangika

Achyutashtakam

Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Vishnu / Krishna

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Eight verses made almost entirely of the loving names of Vishnu and Krishna — Achyuta, Keshava, Rama, Narayana, Govinda. The first verse, 'Achyutam Keshavam Rama Narayanam', is sung as a bhajan everywhere.

When to recite

Ekadashi, Saturdays, Janmashtami and Vaikuntha Ekadashi; morning or evening prayer.

The stotram

Verse 1
acyutaṃ keśavaṃ rāmanārāyaṇaṃ kṛṣṇadāmodaraṃ vāsudevaṃ harim | śrīdharaṃ mādhavaṃ gopikāvallabhaṃ jānakīnāyakaṃ rāmacandraṃ bhaje || 1 ||
Meaning: I worship Achyuta, Keshava, Rama, Narayana, Krishna, Damodara, Vasudeva, Hari, Shridhara, Madhava, the beloved of the gopis, and Ramachandra, the Lord of Janaki.
Verse 2
acyutaṃ keśavaṃ satyabhāmādhavaṃ mādhavaṃ śrīdharaṃ rādhikārādhitam | indirāmandiraṃ cetasā sundaraṃ devakīnandanaṃ nandajaṃ sandadhe || 2 ||
Meaning: I hold in my heart Achyuta, Keshava, husband of Satyabhama, Madhava, Shridhara, worshipped by Radha, home of Lakshmi, beautiful to the mind, son of Devaki and of Nanda.
Verse 3
viṣṇave jiṣṇave śaṅkhine cakriṇe rukmiṇirāgiṇe jānakījānaye | vallavīvallabhāyārcitāyātmane kaṃsavidhvaṃsine vaṃśine te namaḥ || 3 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Vishnu the victorious, who holds the conch and the discus, loved by Rukmini, husband of Janaki, dear to the cowherd women, the worshipped Self, destroyer of Kamsa, player of the flute.
Verse 4
kṛṣṇa govinda he rāma nārāyaṇa śrīpate vāsudevājita śrīnidhe | acyutānanta he mādhavādhokṣaja dvārakānāyaka draupadīrakṣaka || 4 ||
Meaning: O Krishna, Govinda, Rama, Narayana, Lord of Lakshmi, Vasudeva, unconquered, treasure of wealth, Achyuta, endless one, Madhava, Adhokshaja, Lord of Dwaraka, protector of Draupadi!
Verse 5
rākṣasakṣobhitaḥ sītayā śobhito daṇḍakāraṇyabhūpuṇyatākāraṇaḥ | lakṣmaṇenānvito vānaraiḥ sevito 'gastyasampūjito rāghavaḥ pātu mām || 5 ||
Meaning: May Raghava protect me — who stirred up the demons, shone beside Sita, made the Dandaka forest holy, was joined by Lakshmana, served by the monkeys and worshipped by Agastya.
Verse 6
dhenukāriṣṭako'niṣṭakṛddveṣihā keśihā kaṃsahṛdvaṃśikāvādakaḥ | pūtanākopakaḥ sūrajākhelano bālagopālakaḥ pātu māṃ sarvadā || 6 ||
Meaning: May the child-cowherd always protect me — who ended Dhenuka, Arishta and other evil ones, killed Keshi and Kamsa, played the flute, punished Putana and played on the banks of the Yamuna.
Verse 7
vidyududyotavatprasphuradvāsasaṃ prāvṛḍambhodavatprollasadvigraham | vanyayā mālayā śobhitoraḥsthalaṃ lohitāṅghridvayaṃ vārijākṣaṃ bhaje || 7 ||
Meaning: I worship the lotus-eyed Lord whose yellow cloth flashes like lightning, whose body glows like a rain cloud, whose chest shines with a garland of forest flowers, and whose feet are reddish.
Verse 8
kuñcitaiḥ kuntalairbhrājamānānanaṃ ratnamauliṃ lasatkuṇḍalaṃ gaṇḍayoḥ | hārakeyūrakaṃ kaṅkaṇaprojjvalaṃ kiṅkiṇīmañjulaṃ śyāmalaṃ taṃ bhaje || 8 ||
Meaning: I worship that dark-blue Lord whose face shines with curly hair, who wears a jewelled crown and earrings on his cheeks, a necklace, armlets and bright bangles, and sweet tinkling anklets.
Closing verse
acyutasyāṣṭakaṃ yaḥ paṭhediṣṭadaṃ premataḥ pratyahaṃ pūruṣaḥ saspṛham | vṛttataḥ sundaraṃ kartṛ viśvambhara- stasya vaśyo harirjāyate satvaram ||
Meaning: Whoever lovingly recites this wish-fulfilling Achyutashtakam every day, with longing, soon wins the heart of Hari, the beautiful sustainer of the universe.
