Kalabhairava Ashtakam

Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Kala Bhairava

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Eight powerful verses to Kala Bhairava, the fierce form of Shiva and guardian of Kashi. Every verse ends 'Kashika-puradhinatha Kalabhairavam bhaje' — I worship Kalabhairava, Lord of Kashi.

When to recite

Krishna Ashtami every month (Kalashtami), Kala Bhairava Jayanti, Sundays and Tuesdays.

Meaning in simple words

Shankaracharya describes Bhairava: his feet are worshipped by Indra; he wears the moon and serpents; he is the light of crores of suns, three-eyed, dark-throated, holding the trident, noose and skull-staff; he is the lord of time, rides a dog, and protects his devotees from sin and fear. He is gentle to the good and terrible to evil. The closing verse says that those who recite it gain knowledge and merit, and their grief, delusion, poverty, anger and fear are removed — they reach the feet of Kalabhairava.

The stotram

Verse 1
devarājasevyamānapāvanāṅghripaṅkajaṃ vyālayajñasūtraminduśekharaṃ kṛpākaram | nāradādiyogivṛndavanditaṃ digambaraṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 1 ||
Verse 2
bhānukoṭibhāsvaraṃ bhavābdhitārakaṃ paraṃ nīlakaṇṭhamīpsitārthadāyakaṃ trilocanam | kālakālamambujākṣamakṣaśūlamakṣaraṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 2 ||
Verse 3
śūlaṭaṅkapāśadaṇḍapāṇimādikāraṇaṃ śyāmakāyamādidevamakṣaraṃ nirāmayam | bhīmavikramaṃ prabhuṃ vicitratāṇḍavapriyaṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 3 ||
Verse 4
bhuktimuktidāyakaṃ praśastacāruvigrahaṃ bhaktavatsalaṃ sthitaṃ samastalokavigraham | vinikvaṇanmanojñahemakiṅkiṇīlasatkaṭiṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 4 ||
Verse 5
dharmasetupālakaṃ tvadharmamārganāśakaṃ karmapāśamocakaṃ suśarmadāyakaṃ vibhum | svarṇavarṇaśeṣapāśaśobhitāṅgamaṇḍalaṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 5 ||
Verse 6
ratnapādukāprabhābhirāmapādayugmakaṃ nityamadvitīyamiṣṭadaivataṃ nirañjanam | mṛtyudarpanāśanaṃ karāladaṃṣṭramokṣadaṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 6 ||
Verse 7
aṭṭahāsabhinnapadmajāṇḍakośasantatiṃ dṛṣṭipātanaṣṭapāpajālamugraśāsanam | aṣṭasiddhidāyakaṃ kapālamālikādharaṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 7 ||
Verse 8
bhūtasaṅghanāyakaṃ viśālakīrtidāyakaṃ kāśivāsalokapuṇyapāpaśodhakaṃ vibhum | nītimārgakovidaṃ purātanaṃ jagatpatiṃ kāśikāpurādhināthakālabhairavaṃ bhaje || 8 ||
Closing verse
kālabhairavāṣṭakaṃ paṭhanti ye manoharaṃ jñānamuktisādhanaṃ vicitrapuṇyavardhanam | śokamohadainyalobhakopatāpanāśanaṃ prayānti kālabhairavāṅghrisannidhiṃ narā dhruvam ||
