Shiva Manasa Puja

Adi Shankaracharya · Worship of Shiva in the mind

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

A beautiful puja done entirely in the mind: a jewelled seat, Ganga water, fine clothes, flowers, lamps, food and music — all offered to Shiva in imagination. It teaches that sincere devotion matters more than material things.

When to recite

Any time — especially when you cannot do a full puja (travelling, unwell, no time). Mondays and Shivaratri.

Meaning in simple words

Verse 1: I have imagined a jewelled seat, cool bath water, divine clothes, sandal paste, flowers, incense and a lamp — please accept them. Verse 2: I offer sweet rice, milk dishes, fruits, betel and camphor arati, all made in my mind. Verse 3: I offer an umbrella, fans, a mirror, music, dance and prostrations. Verse 4: My self is you, my mind is Parvati, my breath your attendants, my body your temple; my sleep is samadhi, my walking is circling you, my words are your praise — whatever I do is your worship. Verse 5: Forgive all the wrongs I have done with my hands, feet, words, body, ears, eyes or mind, knowingly or unknowingly.
