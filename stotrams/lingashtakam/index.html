Lingashtakam

Traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Shiva

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Eight short verses in praise of the Shiva Linga. Every verse ends with the same line, 'I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga', which makes it easy for children and beginners to learn.

When to recite

Mondays, Pradosham, Masa Shivaratri, Maha Shivaratri and all through Karthika month — during Shiva puja or when visiting a Shiva temple.

The stotram

Verse 1
brahma murāri surārcita liṅgaṃ nirmala bhāsita śobhita liṅgam | janmaja duḥkha vināśaka liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 1 ||
Meaning: Worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu and the gods; pure, radiant and beautiful; it removes the sorrows that come with birth. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 2
devamuni pravarārcita liṅgaṃ kāmadahana karuṇākara liṅgam | rāvaṇa darpa vināśana liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 2 ||
Meaning: Honoured by the gods and the great sages; it burnt Kama (desire), is full of compassion, and humbled the pride of Ravana. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 3
sarva sugandhi sulepita liṅgaṃ buddhi vivardhana kāraṇa liṅgam | siddha surāsura vandita liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 3 ||
Meaning: Anointed with every fragrant paste; it helps the mind grow in wisdom; siddhas, gods and asuras all bow to it. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 4
kanaka mahāmaṇi bhūṣita liṅgaṃ phaṇipati veṣṭita śobhita liṅgam | dakṣa suyajña vināśana liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 4 ||
Meaning: Adorned with gold and precious gems, beautifully encircled by the king of serpents; it ended Daksha's proud sacrifice. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 5
kuṅkuma candana lepita liṅgaṃ paṅkaja hāra suśobhita liṅgam | sañcita pāpa vināśana liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 5 ||
Meaning: Smeared with kumkum and sandal, made beautiful with lotus garlands; it destroys the sins we have gathered. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 6
devagaṇārcita sevita liṅgaṃ bhāvairbhaktibhireva ca liṅgam | dinakara koṭi prabhākara liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 6 ||
Meaning: Worshipped and served by the hosts of gods with love and devotion; it shines like crores of suns. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 7
aṣṭadalopari veṣṭita liṅgaṃ sarva samudbhava kāraṇa liṅgam | aṣṭa daridra vināśita liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 7 ||
Meaning: Seated on an eight-petalled lotus, it is the cause from which everything arises; it removes the eight kinds of poverty. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Verse 8
suraguru suravara pūjita liṅgaṃ suravana puṣpa sadārcita liṅgam | parātparaṃ paramātmaka liṅgaṃ tat praṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam || 8 ||
Meaning: Worshipped by Brihaspati, guru of the gods, and by the best of the gods with flowers from the heavenly gardens; it is the highest of the high, the Supreme Self. I bow to that eternal Shiva Linga.
Closing verse
liṅgāṣṭakamidaṃ puṇyaṃ yaḥ paṭhecchivasannidhau | śivalokamavāpnoti śivena saha modate ||
Meaning: Whoever recites this holy Lingashtakam near Shiva reaches Shiva's world and rejoices with Shiva.
