Sri Ramachandra Kripalu Bhajamana

Goswami Tulsidas (Vinaya Patrika) · Hymn to Lord Rama

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

A much-loved song by Tulsidas in old Hindi (Awadhi), asking the mind to worship gentle, merciful Rama — the lotus-eyed Lord who removes the fear of this world.

When to recite

Sri Rama Navami, Thursdays and Saturdays, and in evening bhajans.

Meaning in simple words

O mind, worship merciful Ramachandra, who removes the great fear of worldly life. His eyes, face, hands and feet are like fresh lotuses. His beauty is greater than countless Kamadevas; his body is like a fresh blue cloud; he wears yellow silk shining like lightning. He is the friend of the poor, the sun of the Raghu dynasty, the destroyer of demons, the joy of Kaushalya and of the people of Kosala. He wears a crown, earrings and a tilak; his arms reach his knees; he holds the bow and arrows and won the battle against Khara and Dushana. Tulsidas prays: O Lord who delights the hearts of Shiva, Shesha and the sages, live in the lotus of my heart and destroy all my faults.
