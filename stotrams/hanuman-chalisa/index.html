Hanuman Chalisa

Goswami Tulsidas · Forty verses in praise of Lord Hanuman (in Awadhi)

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The most widely recited prayer to Hanuman — two opening dohas, forty chaupais and a closing doha. It tells his story and his qualities: strength, wisdom, devotion to Rama and protection of devotees.

When to recite

Tuesdays and Saturdays, Hanuman Jayanti, before travel, and whenever you feel afraid or need courage.

Meaning in simple words

Tulsidas first cleans the mirror of his mind with the dust of his Guru's feet and asks Hanuman for strength, wisdom and knowledge. He then praises Hanuman as an ocean of wisdom, Rama's messenger, son of Anjana and the Wind god, mighty yet gentle. The verses recall his deeds: he crossed the ocean, found Sita, burnt Lanka, brought the Sanjeevani herb to save Lakshmana, and was embraced by Rama as dear as Bharata. Hanuman guards Rama's door; through him we reach Rama. Ghosts and evil cannot come near those who chant his name; illness and pain go away; he rescues those who remember him in thought, word and deed. Whoever recites the Chalisa a hundred times is freed from bondage and gains great joy. The closing doha asks Hanuman, with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita, to live in our hearts.

The stotram

Verse 1
śrīguru carana saroja raja nija manu mukuru sudhāri | baranau~ raghubara bimala jasu jo dāyaku phala cāri || buddhihīna tanu jānike sumirauṃ pavana-kumāra | bala budhi bidyā dehu mohiṃ harahu kalesa bikāra ||
Verse 2
jaya hanumāna jñāna guna sāgara | jaya kapīsa tihu~ loka ujāgara || rāma dūta atulita bala dhāmā | añjani-putra pavanasuta nāmā ||
Verse 3
mahābīra bikrama bajaraṅgī | kumati nivāra sumati ke saṅgī || kañcana barana birāja subesā | kānana kuṇḍala kuñcita kesā ||
Verse 4
hātha bajra au dhvajā birājai | kā~dhe mū~ja janeū sājai || saṅkara suvana kesarīnandana | teja pratāpa mahā jaga bandana ||
Verse 5
bidyāvāna gunī ati cātura | rāma kāja karibe ko ātura || prabhu caritra sunibe ko rasiyā | rāma lakhana sītā mana basiyā ||
Verse 6
sūkṣma rūpa dhari siyahiṃ dikhāvā | bikaṭa rūpa dhari laṅka jarāvā || bhīma rūpa dhari asura sa~hāre | rāmacandra ke kāja sa~vāre ||
Verse 7
lāya sajīvana lakhana jiyāye | śrīraghubīra haraṣi ura lāye || raghupati kīnhī bahuta baḍāī | tuma mama priya bharatahi sama bhāī ||
Verse 8
sahasa badana tumharo jasa gāvaiṃ | asa kahi śrīpati kaṇṭha lagāvaiṃ || sanakādika brahmādi munīsā | nārada sārada sahita ahīsā ||
Verse 9
jama kubera digapāla jahā~ te | kabi kobida kahi sake kahā~ te || tuma upakāra sugrīvahiṃ kīnhā | rāma milāya rāja pada dīnhā ||
Verse 10
tumharo mantra bibhīṣana mānā | laṅkesvara bhae saba jaga jānā || juga sahasra jojana para bhānū | līlyo tāhi madhura phala jānū ||
Verse 11
prabhu mudrikā meli mukha māhīṃ | jaladhi lā~ghi gaye acaraja nāhīṃ || durgama kāja jagata ke jete | sugama anugraha tumhare tete ||
Verse 12
rāma duāre tuma rakhavāre | hota na ājñā binu paisāre || saba sukha lahai tumhārī saranā | tuma racchaka kāhū ko ḍara nā ||
Verse 13
āpana teja samhāro āpai | tīnoṃ loka hā~ka teṃ kā~pai || bhūta pisāca nikaṭa nahiṃ āvai | mahābīra jaba nāma sunāvai ||
Verse 14
nāsai roga harai saba pīrā | japata nirantara hanumata bīrā || saṅkaṭa teṃ hanumāna chuḍāvai | mana krama bacana dhyāna jo lāvai ||
Verse 15
saba para rāma tapasvī rājā | tina ke kāja sakala tuma sājā || aura manoratha jo koī lāvai | soi amita jīvana phala pāvai ||
Verse 16
cāroṃ juga paratāpa tumhārā | hai parasiddha jagata ujiyārā || sādhu santa ke tuma rakhavāre | asura nikandana rāma dulāre ||
Verse 17
aṣṭa siddhi nau nidhi ke dātā | asa bara dīna jānakī mātā || rāma rasāyana tumhare pāsā | sadā raho raghupati ke dāsā ||
Verse 18
tumhare bhajana rāma ko pāvai | janama janama ke dukha bisarāvai || anta kāla raghubara pura jāī | jahā~ janma hari-bhakta kahāī ||
Verse 19
aura devatā citta na dharaī | hanumata sei sarba sukha karaī || saṅkaṭa kaṭai miṭai saba pīrā | jo sumirai hanumata balabīrā ||
Verse 20
jai jai jai hanumāna gosāīṃ | kṛpā karahu gurudeva kī nāīṃ || jo sata bāra pāṭha kara koī | chūṭahi bandi mahā sukha hoī ||
Verse 21
jo yaha paḍhai hanumāna cālīsā | hoya siddhi sākhī gaurīsā || tulasīdāsa sadā hari cerā | kījai nātha hṛdaya maha~ ḍerā ||
Verse 22
pavanatanaya saṅkaṭa harana maṅgala mūrati rūpa | rāma lakhana sītā sahita hṛdaya basahu sura bhūpa ||
