Shiva Panchakshara Stotram

Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Shiva

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Five verses, one for each sacred syllable of 'Na-Ma-Shi-Va-Ya'. Each verse starts with that syllable, so singing it is like chanting the Panchakshari mantra slowly with its meaning.

When to recite

Mondays, Pradosham, Shivaratri and Karthika month; any time you chant 'Om Namah Shivaya'.

The stotram

Verse 1
nāgendrahārāya trilocanāya bhasmāṅgarāgāya maheśvarāya | nityāya śuddhāya digambarāya tasmai nakārāya namaḥ śivāya || 1 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Shiva, the syllable 'Na' — who wears the king of serpents as a garland, has three eyes, is smeared with sacred ash, the great Lord, eternal, pure, with the directions as his clothes.
Verse 2
mandākinīsalilacandanacarcitāya nandīśvarapramathanāthamaheśvarāya | mandāramukhyabahupuṣpasupūjitāya tasmai makārāya namaḥ śivāya || 2 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Shiva, the syllable 'Ma' — anointed with Ganga water and sandal, Lord of Nandi and of all the ganas, worshipped with mandara and many other flowers.
Verse 3
śivāya gaurīvadanābjavṛnda- sūryāya dakṣādhvaranāśakāya | śrīnīlakaṇṭhāya vṛṣadhvajāya tasmai śikārāya namaḥ śivāya || 3 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Shiva, the syllable 'Shi' — the auspicious one, the sun that makes the lotus face of Gauri bloom, who ended Daksha's sacrifice, the blue-throated one whose flag bears the bull.
Verse 4
vasiṣṭhakumbhodbhavagautamārya- munīndradevārcitaśekharāya | candrārkavaiśvānaralocanāya tasmai vakārāya namaḥ śivāya || 4 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Shiva, the syllable 'Va' — whose head is worshipped by Vasishtha, Agastya, Gautama and the great sages and gods, whose three eyes are the moon, the sun and fire.
Verse 5
yakṣasvarūpāya jaṭādharāya pinākahastāya sanātanāya | divyāya devāya digambarāya tasmai yakārāya namaḥ śivāya || 5 ||
Meaning: Salutations to Shiva, the syllable 'Ya' — who takes the form of a yaksha, wears matted hair, holds the Pinaka bow, the eternal, divine god with the sky as his clothing.
Closing verse
pañcākṣaramidaṃ puṇyaṃ yaḥ paṭhecchivasannidhau | śivalokamavāpnoti śivena saha modate ||
Meaning: Whoever recites this holy Panchakshara hymn near Shiva reaches Shiva's world and rejoices with Shiva.
