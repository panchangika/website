Ganesha Pancharatnam

Adi Shankaracharya · Hymn to Lord Ganesha

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

Five 'gems' of verses praising Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. It begins with the famous line 'Mudakaratta modakam' — Ganesha holding sweet modakas in his hand.

When to recite

Every morning, on Wednesdays, on Sankashti Chaturthi and Vinayaka Chavithi, and before starting any new work or study.

The stotram

Verse 1
mudākarāttamodakaṃ sadā vimuktisādhakaṃ kalādharāvataṃsakaṃ vilāsilokarakṣakam | anāyakaikanāyakaṃ vināśitebhadaityakaṃ natāśubhāśunāśakaṃ namāmi taṃ vināyakam || 1 ||
Meaning: I bow to Vinayaka, who joyfully holds modakas in his hand, who always leads us to freedom, who wears the crescent moon, who protects the world, who is the one leader of those with no other leader, who destroyed the elephant-demon, and who quickly removes the troubles of those who bow to him.
Verse 2
natetarātibhīkaraṃ navoditārkabhāsvaraṃ namatsurārinirjaraṃ natādhikāpaduddharam | sureśvaraṃ nidhīśvaraṃ gajeśvaraṃ gaṇeśvaraṃ maheśvaraṃ tamāśraye parātparaṃ nirantaram || 2 ||
Meaning: I always take refuge in that Lord of the ganas, who is fearsome to the proud, bright as the rising sun, honoured by gods and demons alike, who lifts his devotees out of great dangers — lord of the gods, of wealth, of elephants and of all beings, higher than the highest.
Verse 3
samastalokaśaṅkaraṃ nirastadaityakuñjaraṃ daretarodaraṃ varaṃ varebhavaktramakṣaram | kṛpākaraṃ kṣamākaraṃ mudākaraṃ yaśaskaraṃ manaskaraṃ namaskṛtāṃ namaskaromi bhāsvaram || 3 ||
Meaning: I bow to the shining one who brings good to all the worlds, who struck down the demon-elephant, with a big belly and an elephant face, imperishable, full of compassion, patience and joy, who gives fame and a good mind to those who worship him.
Verse 4
akiñcanārtimārjanaṃ cirantanoktibhājanaṃ purāripūrvanandanaṃ surārigarvacarvaṇam | prapañcanāśabhīṣaṇaṃ dhanañjayādibhūṣaṇaṃ kapoladānavāraṇaṃ bhaje purāṇavāraṇam || 4 ||
Meaning: I worship that ancient elephant-faced god who wipes away the sorrows of the poor, praised in the old scriptures, the eldest son of Shiva, who crushed the pride of the demons, fearsome at the end of the world, adorned with serpents.
Verse 5
nitāntakāntadantakāntimantakāntakātmajaṃ acintyarūpamantahīnamantarāyakṛntanam | hṛdantare nirantaraṃ vasantameva yogināṃ tamekadantameva taṃ vicintayāmi santatam || 5 ||
Meaning: I meditate always on the single-tusked one, the son of Shiva, whose tusk shines with great beauty, whose form is beyond thought and without end, who cuts away every obstacle and lives forever in the hearts of yogis.
Closing verse
mahāgaṇeśapañcaratnamādareṇa yo'nvahaṃ pragāyati prabhātake hṛdi smaran gaṇeśvaram | arogatāmadoṣatāṃ susāhitīṃ suputratāṃ samāhitāyuraṣṭabhūtimabhyupaiti so'cirāt ||
Meaning: Whoever sings these five gems every morning with respect, remembering Ganesha in the heart, soon gains good health, a faultless life, learning, good children, a long and steady life and all eight kinds of prosperity.
