Saraswati Stotram (Ya Kundendu)

Traditional prayer · Hymn to Goddess Saraswati

Read in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The best-known prayer to Saraswati, the goddess of learning, music and speech, with the short student's prayer 'Saraswati namastubhyam'. Many schools begin the day with it.

When to recite

Before studies and exams, on Vasant Panchami, Saraswati Puja (Navaratri) and Vijaya Dashami — a daily prayer for students.

The stotram

Verse 1
yā kundendutuṣārahāradhavalā yā śubhravastrāvṛtā yā vīṇāvaradaṇḍamaṇḍitakarā yā śvetapadmāsanā | yā brahmācyutaśaṅkaraprabhṛtibhirdevaiḥ sadā vanditā sā māṃ pātu sarasvatī bhagavatī niḥśeṣajāḍyāpahā || 1 ||
Meaning: May goddess Saraswati protect me and remove all my dullness — she who is white like the jasmine, the moon, snow and a pearl necklace, dressed in pure white, whose hands are adorned with the veena, who sits on a white lotus, and who is always worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and the gods.
Verse 2
śuklāṃ brahmavicārasāraparamāmādyāṃ jagadvyāpinīṃ vīṇāpustakadhāriṇīmabhayadāṃ jāḍyāndhakārāpahām | haste sphāṭikamālikāṃ vidadhatīṃ padmāsane saṃsthitāṃ vande tāṃ parameśvarīṃ bhagavatīṃ buddhipradāṃ śāradām || 2 ||
Meaning: I bow to Sharada, the Supreme Goddess who gives wisdom — fair and pure, the essence of the knowledge of Brahman, the first one who fills the world, holding the veena and a book, giving freedom from fear, removing the darkness of ignorance, holding a crystal rosary and seated on a lotus.
Verse 3
sarasvati namastubhyaṃ varade kāmarūpiṇi | vidyārambhaṃ kariṣyāmi siddhirbhavatu me sadā || 3 ||
Meaning: O Saraswati, giver of boons, who takes any form she wishes, I bow to you. I am about to begin my studies — may I always succeed.
