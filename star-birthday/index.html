What is a star birthday?

On the day you were born, the Moon was in one of 27 star groups (nakshatras). That is your Janma Nakshatra. In many South Indian homes, your star birthday is the day in your birth month when the Moon is back in that same star at sunrise. Because the Moon's calendar does not follow the English calendar, the date moves every year.

How we work it out

Your birth star comes from the Moon's position at the moment you were born (sidereal, Lahiri ayanamsa, the standard in Indian panchangams). Your star birthday is the day in your birth month whose sunrise, in the town where you live now, falls in that star. Telugu, Kannada and Marathi homes count the birth month by the Moon (lunar month); Tamil homes count it by the Sun (Tamil month). Customs differ from family to family, so check with your elders.
