What Panchangika is

Panchangika is a free daily panchangam for general information and religious observance. The timings are calculated from astronomical positions of the sun and moon for the city named on each card, using the Lahiri ayanamsa, and are checked against standard almanacs. Different traditions and almanacs can differ by a few minutes, and timings change with location.
