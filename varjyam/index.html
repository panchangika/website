Varjyam

Telugu: వర్జ్యం · Tamil: வர்ஜ்யம்

Varjyam (Thyajyam) is a period of about one and a half hours, worked out from the day's nakshatra, that is considered harmful. Auspicious work is not started in it.

How it is worked out

Each nakshatra has a fixed point at which Varjyam begins; the actual clock time depends on when the Moon is in that nakshatra, so it can fall at any hour of the day or night.
