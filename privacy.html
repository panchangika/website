Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Panchangika publishes a free daily panchangam (Hindu almanac) in Telugu, Tamil and English on this website and on Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. This page explains what information we handle and why. In short: we don't ask for your personal details to read the panchangam, and we don't sell anything about you.

This website

The website has no sign-up and no advertising, and sets no cookies. To count visits we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not track individual visitors and does not build profiles; it only gives us totals such as page views, countries, device types and which site a visitor came from. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. If you choose a language, your browser remembers that choice on your own device so the page opens in the same language next time. It is never sent to us. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Our channels on other apps

When you follow Panchangika on Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, that app's own privacy policy applies. These apps show us only general figures such as the number of followers and views. We do not collect or store your phone number, contacts or messages from them. If you write to us directly, we use your message only to reply to you.

YouTube

Panchangika uses YouTube API Services only to upload our own daily videos to our own YouTube channels. We do not use the YouTube API to access, collect or store any information about viewers. By watching our videos on YouTube you are using YouTube under the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or remove any app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Data storage and deletion

Our upload tool stores only the access token for our own three YouTube channels, kept as an encrypted secret in our build system. It does not collect, store or share any YouTube viewer or user data, so there is no user data to delete. If you have written to us and want your email deleted, ask us at panchangika.in@gmail.com and we will delete it within 7 days. Our own authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, after which any stored token is deleted.

Email

If you email panchangika.in@gmail.com, we keep your email address and message only as long as needed to answer you, and we never share them for marketing.

Paid plans

Panchangika may later offer an optional paid personal plan. Before that starts, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what information the plan needs (for example your name and birth details for personal timings) and how payments are handled.

Children

Panchangika is a general almanac for families. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For any question about privacy, write to panchangika.in@gmail.com.
