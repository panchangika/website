The 12 Rasis

Also in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The sky is divided into 12 equal parts of 30°, called rasis. The rasi the Moon is in when you are born is your janma rasi (Moon sign).

  1. 1. Mesha
  2. 2. Vrishabha
  3. 3. Mithuna
  4. 4. Karka
  5. 5. Simha
  6. 6. Kanya
  7. 7. Tula
  8. 8. Vrishchika
  9. 9. Dhanu
  10. 10. Makara
  11. 11. Kumbha
  12. 12. Meena
