Gowri Nalla Neram

Telugu: గౌరి పంచాంగం · Tamil: கௌரி நல்ல நேரம்

Gowri Nalla Neram comes from the Gowri panchangam, widely followed in Tamil Nadu. The day and the night are each divided into eight parts named Amirdham, Uthi, Rogam, Labam, Dhanam, Sugam, Soram and Visham.

How it is worked out

Amirdham, Uthi, Labam, Dhanam and Sugam are the good parts (nalla neram) and suit new work; Rogam, Soram and Visham are avoided. The order of the parts depends on the weekday.
