Gulika Kalam

Telugu: గుళిక కాలం · Tamil: குளிகை

Gulika Kalam (Kuligai in Tamil) is the part of the day ruled by Gulika, the son of Shani. Whatever is started in it is believed to repeat, so families avoid it for funerals or taking loans, while some prefer it for things they would like to repeat.

How it is worked out

It is one of the eight equal parts of the daytime, fixed by the weekday.

Which part of the day, by weekday

Counting the eight equal parts of the daytime from sunrise:

  • Sunday7th part of the daytime
  • Monday6th part of the daytime
  • Tuesday5th part of the daytime
  • Wednesday4th part of the daytime
  • Thursday3rd part of the daytime
  • Friday2nd part of the daytime
  • Saturday1st part of the daytime

A note

These are traditional guidelines. For weddings, griha pravesham and other important events, please confirm the muhurtham with your family priest.
