Abhijit Muhurtham

Telugu: అభిజిత్ ముహూర్తం · Tamil: அபிஜித் முகூர்த்தம்

Abhijit Muhurtham is the muhurtham of about 48 minutes around local midday. It is considered auspicious for most new beginnings, and is traditionally not used on Wednesdays.

How it is worked out

It is the eighth of the fifteen muhurthams of the daytime, centred on local noon, which in India is usually a little after 12 o'clock.
