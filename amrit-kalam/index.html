Amrit Kalam

Telugu: అమృత ఘడియలు · Tamil: அமிர்த காலம்

Amrit Kalam (Amrutha Ghadiyalu) is a favourable period worked out from the day's nakshatra. It is considered good for starting important work and ceremonies.

How it is worked out

Like Varjyam, it is tied to the Moon's position in the nakshatra, so its clock time changes every day.
