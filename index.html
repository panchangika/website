How the times are worked out

Every time is calculated from the real positions of the sun and moon for the city shown, using the Lahiri ayanamsa that most Indian almanacs follow. Each day's figures are checked against standard printed panchangams.

Rahu kalam, Yamagandam and Gulika come from the length of the day between sunrise and sunset, so they shift a little every day and from city to city. For a wedding, griha pravesham or other important event, confirm the muhurtham with your family priest.
