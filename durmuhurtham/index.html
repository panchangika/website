Durmuhurtham

Telugu: దుర్ముహూర్తం · Tamil: துர்முகூர்த்தம்

Durmuhurtham is an inauspicious muhurtham of about 48 minutes that comes once or twice a day depending on the weekday. It is avoided for weddings, griha pravesham and new ventures.

How it is worked out

A muhurtham is one-fifteenth of the daytime (or night-time). The weekday decides which muhurtham is Durmuhurtham.
