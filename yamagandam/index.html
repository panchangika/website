Yamagandam

Telugu: యమగండం · Tamil: எமகண்டம்

Yamagandam (Yamaganda Kalam) is a daily period linked to Yama that is considered unsuitable for auspicious beginnings, especially for setting out on a journey.

How it is worked out

Like Rahu Kalam, it is one of the eight equal parts of the daytime, fixed by the weekday, so its clock time shifts with sunrise and sunset.

Which part of the day, by weekday

Counting the eight equal parts of the daytime from sunrise:

  • Sunday5th part of the daytime
  • Monday4th part of the daytime
  • Tuesday3rd part of the daytime
  • Wednesday2nd part of the daytime
  • Thursday1st part of the daytime
  • Friday7th part of the daytime
  • Saturday6th part of the daytime

A note

These are traditional guidelines. For weddings, griha pravesham and other important events, please confirm the muhurtham with your family priest.
