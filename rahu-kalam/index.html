Rahu Kalam

Telugu: రాహుకాలం · Tamil: ராகு காலம்

Rahu Kalam (also written Rahu Kaal or Rahukalam) is a period of about one and a half hours every day that is traditionally avoided for starting anything new: a journey, a purchase, a business deal or a ceremony.

How it is worked out

The daytime from sunrise to sunset is divided into eight equal parts. Rahu Kalam is one of these parts, fixed by the weekday. Because sunrise and sunset change through the year and from city to city, the clock time of Rahu Kalam changes too, which is why a fixed table like '7:30 to 9:00 on Monday' is only approximate.

Which part of the day, by weekday

Counting the eight equal parts of the daytime from sunrise:

  • Sunday8th part of the daytime
  • Monday2nd part of the daytime
  • Tuesday7th part of the daytime
  • Wednesday5th part of the daytime
  • Thursday6th part of the daytime
  • Friday4th part of the daytime
  • Saturday3rd part of the daytime

A note

These are traditional guidelines. For weddings, griha pravesham and other important events, please confirm the muhurtham with your family priest.
