The 27 Nakshatras

Also in: తెలుగు · தமிழ்

The Moon's path is divided into 27 equal parts of 13°20′, called nakshatras (stars). The nakshatra the Moon is in when you are born is your janma nakshatra, your birth star.

  1. 1. Ashwini
  2. 2. Bharani
  3. 3. Krittika
  4. 4. Rohini
  5. 5. Mrigashira
  6. 6. Ardra
  7. 7. Punarvasu
  8. 8. Pushya
  9. 9. Ashlesha
  10. 10. Magha
  11. 11. Purva Phalguni
  12. 12. Uttara Phalguni
  13. 13. Hasta
  14. 14. Chitra
  15. 15. Swati
  16. 16. Vishakha
  17. 17. Anuradha
  18. 18. Jyeshtha
  19. 19. Mula
  20. 20. Purva Ashadha
  21. 21. Uttara Ashadha
  22. 22. Shravana
  23. 23. Dhanishta
  24. 24. Shatabhisha
  25. 25. Purva Bhadrapada
  26. 26. Uttara Bhadrapada
  27. 27. Revati
