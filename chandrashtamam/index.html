Chandrashtamam

Telugu: చంద్రాష్టమం · Tamil: சந்திராஷ்டமம்

Chandrashtamam is the period of roughly two and a quarter days when the Moon passes through the eighth sign (rasi) from a person's birth rasi. People of that rasi are advised to be careful and avoid big decisions.

How it is worked out

Each day's panchangam names the rasi for which it is Chandrashtamam.
